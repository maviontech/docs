--- SLIDE 12 (layout: DEFAULT) pics=0
  INTEGRITY ENGINE
  Integrity you can put a number on.
  Integrity score
  92  / 100
  Every attempt starts at 100. Weighted risk signals subtract points, producing a defensible score and a low / medium / high / critical risk level.
  Weighted behavioural signals
  Tab switch  -4.2
  Paste attempt  -3.5
  Window blur  -3.2
  Copy attempt  -2.5
  Fullscreen exit  -2.5
  ✓   Works even without a camera — focus, tab and clipboard telemetry score non-proctored exams too.
  ✓   Answer-similarity detection surfaces collusion and AI-written responses across candidates.
  PragyanNxt   ·   Enterprise Assessment & Certification
  NOTES: The integrity engine turns suspicion into a defensible number. Point weights are the real engine values. It scores non-proctored exams from browser telemetry alone and detects collusion/AI answers. 92 is illustrative.
