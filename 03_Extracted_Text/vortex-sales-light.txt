  THE STATUS QUO
  Why workforce platforms break at scale.
  Disconnected systems
  HR, payroll, attendance, and helpdesk live in separate tools that never quite agree.
  Manual reconciliation
  Finance reconciles attendance to payroll every cycle, manually, in spreadsheets.
  Compliance debt
  Statutory rules change faster than legacy systems can adapt to them.
  Poor employee experience
  Five logins, three forms, and a ticket just to update a phone number.
  Hidden cost of ownership
  License sprawl, integration tax, vendor coordination, double data entry.
  No data trust
  Reports do not match across systems. Decisions get delayed by reconciliation.
