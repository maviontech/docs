--- SLIDE 2 (layout: CONTENT_TITLED) pics=4
  THE CHALLENGE
  School groups still run on registers, spreadsheets and WhatsApp
  Fragmented tools
  Admissions in one tool, fees in another, attendance on paper. Nothing reconciles, and every report is a manual export.
  Leadership flies blind
  Principals and group heads wait days for numbers that should be live: attendance, collections, marks, leave.
  Data risk across campuses
  Student records sit in shared spreadsheets and personal phones, with no access control and no audit trail.
  Parents out of the loop
  Updates are scattered across personal WhatsApp groups, paper circulars and phone calls to the front office.
  2
  NOTES: The status quo in most school groups: disconnected tools, delayed visibility for leadership, uncontrolled student data, and parent communication over personal channels. Agastya addresses all four on one platform.

  ARCHITECTURE
  Database-per-school isolation, enforced on every request

  Student mobile app | Expo · iOS / Android
  Webhooks | Razorpay · WhatsApp
  Daphne ASGI | HTTP + WebSockets (Channels)
  Request pipeline
  1  Tenant DB context
  2  Auth + single session
  3  Tenant + subscription gate
  4  RBAC permission check
  5  Tenant DB router
  Control DB | Tenants · plans · billing
  tenant_greenwood | Isolated MySQL database
  tenant_riverdale … | One database per school
  INTEGRATIONS
  Razorpay | Subscription billing
  Meta WhatsApp | Inbox and templates
  Twilio | SMS and OTP
  SMTP email | Resets, welcomes
  ReportLab / Excel | PDF and XLSX exports
  27
  NOTES: Isolation works in two layers: a dedicated database per school, selected per request by middleware and a database router, plus tenant foreign keys on every business model.
