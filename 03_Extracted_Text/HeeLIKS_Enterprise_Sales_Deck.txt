Inventory lives in a spreadsheet, billing on a separate till. On-hand is a guess by evening, and stock-outs are discovered by customers.
Batches and expiry dates sit on cartons, not in the system. Write-offs show up at the quarterly audit instead of 30 days earlier.
The accountant rebuilds tax summaries, credit notes and GSTR-1 from bill books — slow, error-prone and unauditable.
Shared logins, unlimited discounts and no record of who changed a price. Distributors still run orders on paper challans.
Typical pains of stock-driven outlets and distributors, drawn from the product specification (docs/SRS.md §2.2).

### Notes:
Most outlets we meet run stock in a spreadsheet and billing on a separate till. The two drift apart every day, expiry losses surface at audit, GST is rebuilt from paper by the accountant, and everyone shares one login. These four pains are what HeeLIKS is built to remove.
