MavionTech · Brand Guidelines02

1 · Brand foundation

Momentum with Vision

Mission

Automate and simplify operational workflows with secure, extensible enterprise software that empowers teams to focus on what matters most.

Vision

Create intelligent digital ecosystems that help teams move faster, make better decisions, and transform the way organizations operate.

Promise

Production-grade UX, predictable performance, and maintainable integrations, built for real teams solving real problems.

Brand personality

  • Precise. Specific capabilities, named modules, exact numbers with sources.
  • Calm and confident. Generous space, few colours, no hype.
  • Proven. Real screens with demo data, never mock-ups or stock photos.
  • Practical. Built for Indian enterprises: INR, GST, statutory compliance, WhatsApp.

Voice rules

  • Titles state outcomes ("Results day in hours, not weeks"), not module names.
  • One message per slide; sentence case; no full stop at the end of titles.
  • Never invent customers, statistics, testimonials or certifications. See the Claims Policy in the Portfolio Strategy.
  • Write the company as MavionTech; legal entity MavionTech Pvt. Ltd.

Mission, vision, promise and tagline: maviontech.com (Company page), inspected 10 Nov 2026.

2 · Logo

The MavionTech roundel

MavionTech
Momentum with Vision
MavionTech
Momentum with Vision

Rules

  • Primary: amber roundel on Mavion Midnight. Secondary: on white or Paper.
  • Lockup: roundel + "MavionTech" (bold) + "Momentum with Vision". The wordmark is set in type: no wordmark artwork exists yet.
  • Clear space: at least ¼ of the roundel's diameter on all sides.
  • Minimum size: 0.21 in (5.3 mm) in slide footers; 16 px on screen.
  • Endorsement: every product deck carries the lockup on cover and close, and the small roundel + wordmark in the footer.

✕ Recolour, outline or add effects (glow, bevel, heavy shadow).

✕ Stretch, rotate or crop the roundel.

✕ Place it on busy screenshots or mid-tone backgrounds.

✕ Pair it with a product logo larger than the roundel on the cover.

Action required: the only logo file is a 359 px PNG. A vector (SVG/EPS) master and a monochrome version are needed before print production.

Why Calibri

Calibri ships with every Microsoft Office installation on Windows and Mac. Decks therefore render identically for every salesperson and prospect, with no font substitution or text overflow. It is the presentation typeface for all MavionTech decks.

Inter for digital and print

Inter (SIL Open Font License) is the companion face for the website, PDF catalogue and documents, chosen for its similar humanist proportions. Fallback: Calibri, Segoe UI, Arial.

Never

Decorative or script fonts, more than one typeface in a deck, all-caps sentences (only kickers are caps), centred body text.

MavionTech · Brand Guidelines09

6 · Iconography & illustration

Line icons, the Orbit motif, and nothing decorative

Icons

Lucide line icons, 1.75 stroke, drawn in the product accent on a rounded-square chip of the same accent at 12% tint. On dark slides: white icon on a solid accent chip. One icon per idea; never emoji or clip-art.

The Orbit motif

Concentric rings with a few amber nodes, drawn from the circuit ring in the MavionTech roundel. It appears only on dark covers, dividers and the close slide, together with one soft glow in the product accent. It is the only illustrative device: no abstract blobs, waves or stock art.

Imagery

Real product screenshots with realistic demo data are the hero imagery. No stock photography of people, offices or handshakes. Diagrams are built with native shapes in the brand palette so they stay editable.

MavionTech · Brand Guidelines10

6 · Screenshot treatment

The signature frame: real screens, framed with care

  • Frame: white rounded rectangle (radius 0.08 in), 0.75 pt border #DCE3EC, slim chrome bar with three muted window dots and an optional page label, one soft shadow.
  • Crop: top-anchored to the box, never stretched. Hero shots keep the app chrome; close-ups remove the sidebar.
  • Source: at least 1,600 px wide (Agastya captures are 2,400 px).
  • Callouts: numbered amber badges on the screenshot, matched by numbered captions below.
  • Data hygiene: no real people or clients, no "demo"/"seed"/"QA" labels, no empty states, no error pages, no warnings such as "keys missing".
  • Caption every screenshot (11 pt, Slate 500), and mark demo data as demo data.
