What we found

  • HeeLIKS is a multi-tenant inventory, point-of-sale and B2B order-to-cash platform for Indian stock-driven businesses. Its depth (GST, FEFO, ledger, distributor order-to-cash) is fully evidenced by 181 screenshots and a 127-row coverage map.
  • Vyaas AI is an enterprise AI assistant: cited chat over company knowledge, document and image generation, a sandboxed coding agent that opens pull requests, and organisation governance with cost control.
  • Both source decks were produced in their own visual themes (indigo/lavender, dark navy). Neither follows the locked MavionTech system, and many slides shrink 3–4 screenshots to an illegible size.
  • Key claims check out against both code repositories (D:\heeliks, D:\llm, read-only): test counts, reports, plans, gVisor sandbox, BM25 retrieval, encrypted secrets.

What needs a decision before redesign

  • Product accents. Both brand blues sit almost on top of existing accents (Agastya, PragyanNxt, Vortex). Page 12 proposes two new accents.
  • Catalogue claim. The approved catalogue says “a dedicated database per customer in all six products”. Vyaas isolates at organisation level in a shared database, so the line must be qualified when the catalogue grows to eight.
  • Vyaas evidence gap. No screenshot shows the coding agent's pull request, its headline promise. The capability is verified in code; the deck will say so without a screen unless a capture is supplied.
  • heeliks.com was down during the audit; website copy was read from the site templates in the product repository instead.

Recommendation Approve the positioning and outlines on pages 10–11 and the accents on page 12; then build both decks.

HeeLIKS · Positioning and modules

Inventory, POS and distributor order-to-cash on one stock ledger

Positioning

A multi-tenant inventory, point-of-sale and B2B order-to-cash platform for stock-driven Indian businesses. Every purchase, sale, return, transfer and count posts to one append-only stock ledger, in a database that belongs to that customer alone.

Problem it solves

Spreadsheet stock plus a separate till: stock and sales drift apart, expiry is found too late, GST is rebuilt every month, shared logins leave no trail.

Who buys and uses it

  • Buyer: owner or proprietor of an outlet or chain; MD or finance head of a distributor
  • Influencers: store manager, accountant or CA
  • Users: Owner/Admin, Manager, Cashier, Store staff, Auditor, plus the HeeLIKS operator
  • Verticals: pharmacy, retail and fashion, grocery and kirana, bookstores, industrial spares

Commercials (from code and site)

  • Starter ₹999, Growth ₹2,999, Business ₹5,999 a month; quarterly and yearly cycles
  • Perpetual on-premise ₹7.5 L + optional 5% AMC from year two
  • Add-ons ₹599/user and ₹699/outlet a month; 14-day trial; Razorpay

What the coverage file represents

HeeLIKS_coverage.csv is the product team's traceability matrix. Each row ties a module, feature or USP to a persona, a live URL, the screenshot that proves it and the source-deck slide that shows it. 22 modules, 89 features and 16 USPs, all covered. It is identical to HeeLIKS_deck_work/coverage.csv. We will reuse it as the screenshot index for the new deck and catalogue.

Quality

  • 163 of 181 captures are 2400 × 1500; the rest are full-page or A4 exports. All are sharp enough for full-width use.
  • All data is a seeded demo: MediCare Pharmacy, Urban Threads, FreshMart, Page Palace Books and Titan Equipment & Spares. These are fictional workspaces and will be labelled “(demo data)”.

Vyaas AI · Positioning and modules

Cited answers, finished documents and a coding agent, under organisation governance

Positioning

An enterprise AI assistant platform. Teams chat with leading models over their own documents, with a citation on every grounded answer. They generate Word, PowerPoint, Excel and images, and hand engineering tasks to a sandboxed coding agent that commits and opens pull requests. All of it runs under organisation policy, audit and a token-optimisation engine. Tagline: “Answers you can cite. Code you can ship.”

Problem it solves

AI pilots stall: answers without sources, company data in consumer tools with no policy or audit, copilots that suggest lines but don't ship work, and token bills nobody can predict.

Who buys and uses it

  • Buyer: CTO, Head of Engineering, IT Director
  • Users: engineers, analysts and knowledge workers, operations; Owner, Admin and Member roles
  • Market: India-origin, priced in INR and USD (Razorpay and Stripe)

Commercials (from code)

  • 7 tiers: Guest, Free, Plus, Pro, Max, Team (per seat, minimum 3), Enterprise
  • Pro $29 or ₹2,499 a month; Team $25 or ₹2,099 per seat; 2 months free on annual
  • Metered in Sonnet-equivalent tokens; GST at checkout
