3 · Components

Twelve components cover every slide

Kicker + title

Kicker 0.3 in tall at y 0.50; title at y 0.78. No underline or accent bar beneath titles.

Card

Rounded rectangle r 0.10 in, Paper fill (White on tinted slides), 0.75 pt Line border, 0.25 in inner padding. No edge stripes.

Icon chip

0.50–0.56 in rounded square, accent at 12% tint, Lucide icon at 52% of the chip in the full accent.

Screenshot frame

See Brand Guidelines §6. Chrome 0.26 in, inset 0.04 in, one soft shadow (blur 14, offset 4, 12% opacity).

Numbered callout

0.34 in amber circle, Midnight numeral 11 pt bold; repeated on the screenshot and in the caption row.

Pill chip

0.38 in tall full-radius pill; on dark: Midnight Raised fill with #2E4468 border.

Stat tile

Number 44 pt + label 14 pt; one dark tile per row for rhythm; source line required.

Comparison row

Label / Today (muted) / With product (white or Ink, with amber check); 0.75 pt divider lines only.

Buttons (close)

Primary: Amber fill, Midnight text. Secondary: Midnight Raised, white text. 0.5 in tall, full radius.

Tables

Header caps 10 pt Slate 500 with Ink rule; rows 0.75 pt Line dividers; no vertical rules or zebra fills.

Charts

Native PowerPoint charts. Series: accent, then Slate 500, Slate 300. Signal colours for status only. Calibri, no 3D, no chart borders.

Diagrams

Native shapes, r 0.08 in boxes, 1.25 pt Slate 300 connectors with triangle heads; one Midnight block marks the core; amber outline marks the control element.

4 · Header, footer & numbering

Quiet chrome, consistent position

  • No header bar. The kicker and title are the header.
  • Footer left: "Product · Category", 9 pt Slate 500 (on dark: #A9B6C9).
  • Footer right: MavionTech roundel (0.21 in) + "MavionTech" 9 pt bold, then the slide number.
  • Slide number: plain arabic numeral at the right margin, 9 pt. Not shown on covers and close.
  • Sources: 11 pt caption directly above the footer zone, never inside it.
  • Footer, logo and number live on the slide layout, never typed on slides.

7 · Composition rules

How a deck is assembled

Rhythm

  • Sandwich: dark cover → light content → one dark value or roadmap slide per act → dark close.
  • No two neighbouring slides use the same layout; mirror hero layouts left/right.
  • Every content slide has a visual: screenshot, diagram, icon cards or stat tiles. No text-only slides.
  • One message per slide; at most 4 features, 6 cards, 5 process stages.

Text discipline

  • Titles fit on one line at 30 pt (≈ 60 characters).
  • Body ≥ 12.5 pt; no text below 9 pt except legal.
  • Speaker notes (talk track) on every slide.

9 · Production & QA

Built as code, checked before it ships

Build

  • Decks are generated with pptxgenjs from shared tokens (mt.js), so a colour or font change applies everywhere.
  • Screenshots are cropped by script (top-anchored, sidebar crop for close-ups).
  • Icons render from Lucide to 256 px PNG in each accent.

QA checklist

  • Schema validation passes; no folder entries in the zip.
  • Opens in PowerPoint without a repair prompt (verified by COM automation).
  • Rendered and inspected slide by slide: overflow, overlaps, one-line titles, contrast, alignment.
  • Every number has a source; demo data labelled; no unverified claims.
