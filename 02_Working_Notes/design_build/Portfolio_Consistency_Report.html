What we found

  • The six decks already shared one system: layouts, Calibri type, Midnight + Amber, footer, contact card and close.
  • Drift was in the details: section names for the same slide type, closing buttons, demo-data labels, the ₹ symbol, one missing cover mark and one missing architecture diagram.

  • No contradictions between decks on company facts, contact details, claims policy or shared products.

What we did

  • Fixed every finding at the generator level, rebuilt all six decks and re-exported all six PDFs from PowerPoint.
  • Re-measured in PowerPoint: zero overflow; minimum type 9 pt; every screenshot now at least 149 effective DPI.
  • Softened two absolute or comparative lines that could not be evidenced.
  • Recorded intended product differences (trial length, deployment, pricing visibility) so the catalogue can carry them consistently.

Verdict Approved for screen and PDF circulation. Print use waits on new screenshots and vector logos (page 11).

Scope and method

Every slide checked three ways: by script, by measurement and by eye

1 · Content scan

Every text run in all six decks (python-pptx): fonts and sizes, banned and legacy names, claims patterns (uptime, SLA, ISO, SOC 2, GDPR, 24×7, ROI, “trusted by”, awards), US spellings, currency notation, demo-data labels, isolation wording, trial lengths, title length and speaker notes.

2 · PowerPoint measurement

Each deck opened in Microsoft PowerPoint through automation. For every shape: rendered text height against its box (overflow), position against the slide edge, and empty layout placeholders. Effective resolution of every screenshot: pixels against displayed inches.

3 · Visual review

All 152 slides exported from PowerPoint as images and reviewed for clipping, alignment, hierarchy, readability, repetition, screenshot treatment and diagram integrity. Changed slides were re-checked after each rebuild.

On every build: Office schema validator (all passed) · no negative shape extents · no empty placeholders · speaker notes on every slide · editable text, shapes and diagrams (no slide flattened into an image). LibreOffice was not used for any check (decision D5).
